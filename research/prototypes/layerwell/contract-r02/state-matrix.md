## State equivalence

For one frozen build, repeatability means byte equality of canonical event,
state, panel, controller, comparator, and generated PCM artifacts across 16,
64, 128, and 512-frame partitions; exact integer equality of accepted capture
boundaries, recorded lengths, playback offsets, active length, owners, phase,
source advances, panel indices/history, event counts, and diagnostics; and exact
SHA-256 equality of every committed store in trim/rejection/abort preservation
cases.

Freshly reset instances are equivalent when their public Tide Pit and Pamplist
snapshots, Layerwell accepted controls, panel projections, session state,
storage ownership, occupied flags, zeroed committed ranges, and deterministic
source contexts agree. C++ padding, allocator addresses, GUI component state,
host handles, absolute paths, endpoint identities, wall-clock time, unsent
feedback positions, and generated build directories are excluded. Floating
output is compared through the exact generated PCM representation within one
compiler build; cross-toolchain bit identity and audible equivalence are not
claimed.
